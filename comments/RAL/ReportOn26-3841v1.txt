The manuscript proposes VLM-DexSafeDagger, a safety-aware skill
distillation framework that combines teacher-student action
disagreement, a Bellman success-value critic, and VLM-based adaptation
of intervention thresholds for dexterous manipulation.

The reviewers find the safety-oriented framework interesting, but raise
several concerns that should be addressed. Most importantly, both
reviewers question whether the VLM itself provides a clear benefit over
simpler approaches for adaptive threshold tuning. The current
experiments do not sufficiently separate the contribution of the VLM
from the effect of threshold adaptation, and additional comparisons and
ablations are needed to clarify this point.

The reviewers also raise concerns about the safety claims. The current
framework provides empirical intervention rather than a formal safety
guarantee, and unsafe episodes still occur in the real-world
experiments. The scope of the Âsafety-criticalÂ claim should
therefore
be clarified and better supported by the experiments.

Other concerns include the evaluation of the Bellman success-value
critic, reproducibility of the VLM component, the limited number of
real-world trials, and insufficient baseline comparisons. More complete
results for the tested objects and clearer reporting of the
experimental settings are also needed.

These concerns are substantial and should be fully addressed in a
revised manuscript. Without adequately addressing these issues, the
paper would be difficult to consider for publication. Please see the
individual reviews for further details.
