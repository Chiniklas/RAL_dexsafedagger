This paper proposes a framework for distilling privileged RL teacher
policies into an RGB-based student policy. To reduce unsafe exploration
such as collisions during distillation, the authors propose an
intervention gate (DexSafeDagger) that combines a teacher–student
action-disagreement gate with a success-value critic evaluated on the
current student state–action pair through an OR rule, and further
propose adjusting the gate thresholds with a VLM.

The scale of the real-robot evaluation, the reporting of failure cases,
and the strengthened reproducibility material are appreciated, and the
previous revision appropriately moderated the claims regarding the
paper’s main contributions. However, the claimed superiority of the
teacher RL recipe, inconsistencies between the result tables and
figures, and the evidence for the VLM’s contribution remain unresolved,
as detailed below.

Major Comments

M1. The reward for teacher-policy training is described only verbally.
Table I lists only the purpose of each reward term; the exact
formulation, weights, and scales of the terms are not given in the
manuscript. Since teacher training is the starting point of every
subsequent stage, the work does not appear reproducible in its current
form. Please provide the exact formulation and weights of each reward
term.

M2. The claimed superiority of the authors’ RL recipe is not supported.

(1) The authors explain the performance gap between ablations (a) and
(b) by stating that the FABRICS controller was tuned for the original
platform and transfers poorly to the new hardware. This implies that
the DexTrAH baseline was not tuned for the authors’ hardware, and
therefore the comparison cannot serve as evidence that the authors’
recipe produces more effective teacher policies. A comparison against a
DexTrAH baseline re-tuned for the authors’ platform, or additional
experiments and analysis that can demonstrate the superiority of the
recipe, is required.

(2) The difference between the “DexTrAH-RGB-style RL recipe” and “our
RL recipe” is not defined in the manuscript. Please state explicitly
how the two recipes differ.

(3) The second contribution claims “a safety-aware RL reward design
that explicitly accounts for risky events.” However, the reward terms
in Table I are generic shaped-reward terms, and no term explicitly
penalizes the unsafe events defined by the authors, such as collisions
or object escape. Early termination does penalize unsafe events
implicitly by truncating the future return, but it is indistinguishable
from a timeout unless truncation is bootstrapped. The manuscript does
not specify any of these.  Please either add an ablation of the reward
terms or of the termination conditions, or moderate the contribution
statement.

M3. The unsafe rates in Fig. 3 and Table III are inconsistent. In the
“Comparison Unsafe Episode Rate” panel of Fig. 3, (c) is the highest,
(b) and (e) are nearly indistinguishable, and (f) is the lowest. In
contrast, Table III reports (b) 17.4 > (c) 16.7 > (e) 11.9 > (f) 9.3,
which differs in both values and ordering. Please revise so that the
two results are consistent with each other.

M4. The contribution of the VLM is not established by the (e) vs. (f)
comparison alone. In particular, the third contribution claims
effectiveness “across different visual contexts and unsafe scenarios,”
but the results currently presented do not clearly verify this claim.
Please either moderate the corresponding contribution statement or
provide additional analysis.

Minor Comments

m1. One paragraph in Sec. III-A has a line spacing that differs from
the rest of the text. Please check the formatting of the entire
manuscript.

m2. The “Mean” columns of Table II and Table III appear to average over
different populations. Please unify the definition of “Mean” across the
two tables. In addition, please briefly state why these four objects
were selected from the 40-object set.

m3. Fig. 4 is difficult to read at print quality. Please also explain
why only two objects (windcart and cow) are shown.
