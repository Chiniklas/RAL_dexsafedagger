The revised manuscript has improved considerably, and the reviewers
acknowledge the authors' efforts in addressing the previous comments.
However, several important concerns remain, particularly regarding the
RL teacher-training procedure, the fairness of the baseline comparison,
inconsistencies in the reported results, and the evidence supporting
the contribution of VLM-guided adaptation.  These issues need to be
addressed to properly support the claimed contributions. Please see the
individual reviews for further details.
