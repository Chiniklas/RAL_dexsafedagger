\subsection{Teacher-Training Recipe Scope}
\label{app:recipe_comparison}

The submitted DexTrAH/FABRICS baseline was adapted and retuned for the
Tiangong~2 Pro and InspireHand, including the robot geometry, constraints,
action interface, and controller parameters. It retained DexTrAH's
human-motion-derived PCA hand-action manifold and the FABRICS controller between
the RL policy and the robot joints. Our retained teacher implementation instead
follows the normalized direct motor-command formulation used in
\href{https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/html/Wan_UniDexGrasp_Improving_Dexterous_Grasping_Policy_Learning_via_Geometry-Aware_Curriculum_and_ICCV_2023_paper.html}{UniDexGrasp++ (Wan et al., ICCV 2023)}:
it removes FABRICS and maps 13 normalized policy outputs one-to-one to
joint-position targets for the seven arm and six hand DoFs. In our
platform-specific comparison, this direct-control recipe achieved at least
comparable task performance while avoiding human-motion retargeting, PCA
action-manifold construction, and embodiment-specific FABRICS tuning. We treat
this as a practical implementation advantage rather than evidence that the
underlying RL algorithm is intrinsically superior. The two FABRICS-based
ablations were therefore removed from the primary contribution argument. All
retained ablations use the same teacher setup and compare only the
student-distillation and intervention mechanisms.
