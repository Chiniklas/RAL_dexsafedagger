In this work, we propose \methodname, which combines teacher--student
disagreement, Bellman success-value prediction, and VLM-guided intervention-policy
adaptation. The proposed method reduces early unsafe episodes by \textbf{80\%}
relative to vanilla DAgger while maintaining comparable lift success. The
Bellman critic reduces the average unsafe-episode rate by
\textbf{7.5 percentage points} relative to disagreement-only SafeDAgger.
VLM-guided adaptation further reduces it by \textbf{4 percentage points}
relative to \dexsafedagger.
The proposed method approaches and, in some cases, surpasses the teacher policy's
safety performance. Successful real-world deployment shows that the resulting
student policy partially bridges the sim-to-real gap.
Future work should develop stronger teachers through safety-aware training
and improve generalization to unseen objects by addressing the
contact-strategy mismatch between simulation and hardware.
